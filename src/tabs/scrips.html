What it works out

Two loops: craft a collectable, turn it in for crafters’ scrips, spend the scrips on materia and sell it. Orange: level 100 collectables, for grade XII materia. Purple: every Rarefied collectable from level 50 to 98, for materia from grade IV to XI.

Reading the table

Revenue/craft is what one collectable’s scrips are worth as materia on your world, using whichever materia that scrip buys sells best for its scrip cost. Profit/craft takes off the materials. The table below prices the materia itself.

Worth knowing

Lower-level Purple collectables give fewer scrips but often cost far less to make, so sort by profit rather than by scrips. In these recipe trees a precraft is bought NQ wherever it is listed or an NPC sells it, and only broken down into its own materials when none are for sale. Crystals are costed but hidden; tick Show crystals to list them.

Scrip yields and materia costs are read from the game's own collectable and scrip shop tables, at top collectability, and rebaked after each patch. "gil/scrip" uses whichever crafter materia that scrip buys sells for the most per scrip on Spriggan, net of tax. Material cost, shards and crystals included, buys each precraft NQ at the cheapest Chaos price where it is listed, and only expands/crafts a precraft when it has no listings; raw mats NQ. Anything an NPC sells for gil all year is bought from the NPC when that is cheaper than the board, or the board has none. Only crafting (Craftsman’s) materia are shown; materia sells NQ. Avg 30d, Trend and Units/day are 30 days of sales on Spriggan, read the same way as every other tab: the median a unit actually sold at, which one joke sale cannot drag the way an average can. Lower-level Purple gives fewer scrips but often cheaper mats — sort by profit to see which actually wins. Data via Universalis, item icons via XIVAPI.
