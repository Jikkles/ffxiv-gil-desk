What it works out

Every craftable intermediate, 900+ of them, as one simple flip: buy its materials on your data centre, craft it once, sell it HQ on your world. No chains of precrafts and no Free Company workshop.

Reading the table

Mat cost buys every material NQ at its cheapest listing, or from an NPC where one sells it for less, shards and crystals included. Pick your Class and a Min lvl and Max lvl to see only what you can make, and open a row for its material list.

Worth knowing

Plenty of intermediates barely sell HQ, so a row with no sales may still move: switch Sell to NQ to find the bulk market. Crystals are costed but left out of each recipe to keep it short; tick Show crystals to list them.

Gil/day = profit/unit × the units that sell a day on Spriggan — a stack of 99 is 99 units and one sale. You capture a slice of that, so it's a ranking not a promise. Sell now = cheapest current Spriggan listing at the chosen quality (⚠ = far from the average, likely a noise listing). Avg 30d = the median sale price over 30 days, weighted by units, with its sale count. Mat cost = buying every material NQ, shards and crystals included, at the cheapest Chaos price, or from an NPC shop where that is cheaper or the board has none, and crafting once; sell defaults to HQ since that's what you craft. Which world each material is cheapest on is in the row's craft tree, under "Buy on". Data via Universalis. Intermediates with thin HQ markets may show no sale — switch Sell to NQ to see the bulk market.
