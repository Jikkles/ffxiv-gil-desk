What it works out

How much gil an hour of gathering one item brings in: an estimate of how many you gather in an hour, times its price after tax. A regular node can be worked all hour, so Per hour is Nodes an hour × Swings a node × Items a swing. A timed node is only up for a short window a few times a day, so it counts one visit for every window in a real hour (an Eorzean day is 70 minutes).

Reading the table

Node says whether the best node is regular or timed, and when a timed one is next up, in real minutes. Gil/hour is Per hour × the price in Price after tax. The Eorzean spawn times are on the hover. Tick Cap at a day’s sales to count no more an hour than your world buys in a whole day.

Worth knowing

The defaults suit a gatherer with decent gear using yield skills; set Your gathering to what you really see and every row follows. Shards and crystals, spearfishing and the Diadem are left out. Hidden items only turn up on some visits (and some need a Folklore book or enough Perception), so they are left out unless you tick Include hidden items, and their Per hour is an overestimate. Per hour for a timed node assumes you catch every window.

Node data from Teamcraft: what each node gives, its level, where it is and when a timed node spawns. An item from several nodes is ranked on its best one: a regular node first, then the timed node that spawns most often. Per hour is an estimate from Your gathering: nodes an hour × swings a node × items a swing for a regular node; for a timed node, swings × items a swing for each spawn window in a real hour (a node spawning twice an Eorzean day is up about 1.7 times an hour). Gil/hour = Per hour × the chosen price × (1 − tax); with Cap at a day’s sales, Per hour is held to Units/day first. Sell now = cheapest NQ listing on Spriggan. Avg 30d, Trend and Units/day are 30 days of sales there, and Gil/day = Avg 30d × (1 − tax) × Units/day, as on every tab. Data via Universalis.
