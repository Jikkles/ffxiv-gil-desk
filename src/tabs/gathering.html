What it works out

How much gil an hour of gathering one item brings in: an estimate of how many you gather in an hour, times its price after tax. A regular node can be worked all hour, so Per hour is Nodes an hour × Swings a node × Items a swing. A timed node is only up for a short window a few times a day, so it counts one visit for every window in a real hour (an Eorzean day is 70 minutes). Fish are ranked on what sells: how often each one bites at its spot is not known, so Per hour and Gil/hour are left blank for them.

Reading the table

Node says whether the best node or fishing spot is regular or timed, and when a timed one is next up, in real time; a fish’s next window comes from the weather forecast. Gil/hour is Per hour × the price in Price after tax. The Eorzean spawn times, or a fish’s hours and weather, are on the hover. Tick Cap at a day’s sales to count no more an hour than your world buys in a whole day.

Worth knowing

The defaults suit a gatherer with decent gear using yield skills; set Your gathering to what you really see and every row follows. Shards and crystals, spearfishing, ocean fishing, Cosmic Exploration and the Diadem are left out. Big fish that need other fish caught first say so on the hover. Hidden items only turn up on some visits (and some need a Folklore book or enough Perception), so they are left out unless you tick Include hidden items, and their Per hour is an overestimate. Per hour for a timed node assumes you catch every window.

Node and fishing spot data from Teamcraft: what each node gives, its level, where it is and when a timed node spawns. Fish hours, weather and big fish from the Carbuncle Plushy fish tracker, with each zone’s weather odds to forecast the next window. An item from several nodes is ranked on its best one: a regular node first, then the timed node that spawns most often. Per hour is an estimate from Your gathering: nodes an hour × swings a node × items a swing for a regular node; for a timed node, swings × items a swing for each spawn window in a real hour (a node spawning twice an Eorzean day is up about 1.7 times an hour). Gil/hour = Per hour × the chosen price × (1 − tax). Both are left blank for fish, whose bite rates are unknown; with Cap at a day’s sales, Per hour is held to Units/day first. Sell now = cheapest NQ listing on Spriggan. Avg 30d, Trend and Units/day are 30 days of sales there, and Gil/day = Avg 30d × (1 − tax) × Units/day — how much gil the item moves on your world, not your cut of it. The crafting tabs show Profit/day instead, which is your cut. Data via Universalis, item icons via XIVAPI.
