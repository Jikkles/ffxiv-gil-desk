What it works out

What the four 18-hour exploration ventures (Field, Highland, Woodland and Waterside) bring back that is worth selling: mostly minions, plus a few pricey furnishings.

Reading the table

Venture and Tier(s) say which venture brings it back and at what level; a higher tier needs a higher-level retainer. The percentage beside the tiers is how often it comes back at its best tier, from players’ logged ventures. Only from this venture marks a drop with no other source anywhere in the game.

Worth knowing

These sell a handful of times a month on any one world, so Sell now is your world and the rest is your data centre. Tick Venture-exclusive only for the drops you cannot get elsewhere, or Only what is actually selling to drop anything unsold for 30 days. Hide ⚠ outliers also drops rows whose listing sits far from what has sold.
