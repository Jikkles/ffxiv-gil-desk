What it works out

A list you fill yourself. Press 📋 on any row of any tab, or on a material inside a crafting tree, and pick this list: the item lands here with live prices and, for a craft, its whole recipe.

Reading the table

The columns match the Dashboard: sell now, 30-day average, material cost, profit, margin, units and gil a day. Open a row for its crafting tree. A raw material is still priced, just with no crafting cost. A material an NPC sells for less than the board is bought from the NPC. Shards and crystals are costed but hidden; tick Show crystals to list them.

Worth knowing

✎ Rename gives the list its own name, ＋ List in the tab bar makes another (up to five), Clear list empties it with an undo, and ✕ Remove list deletes the tab. Lists are kept in this browser only and never uploaded.

Sell now = cheapest current listing on your chosen world; ⚠ = far from the 30-day average, so the listing is likely noise. Avg 30d = median price of real sales over the past month, weighted by units, so one sale at a joke price cannot drag it (sale count shown). Units/day = how many units the market absorbs a day, so a stack of 99 counts as 99 rather than as one sale, and Gil/day is profit/unit × that — a ranking device rather than a promise. Mat cost defaults to Precraft-optimised: any intermediate cheaper to craft than to buy is costed as crafted, all the way down. Switch it to Buy all mats to price every direct material straight off the board at the cheapest Chaos price instead. An item saved without a recipe is still priced, but shows no crafting cost.
