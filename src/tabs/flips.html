What it works out

No crafting: world-to-world trading on mounts, minions, hairstyles, outfit coffers, emotes, orchestrion rolls, facewear, fashion accessories such as parasols and wings, and General-purpose Pure White and Jet Black dye. Rolls, facewear and accessories are only listed if they average 50k or more in both Europe and North America. It finds the cheapest listing on any world in Buy from and compares it with what the item really sells for on your world.

Reading the table

Buy names the world to buy on: orange means you will have to travel, teal means it is already on yours. Profit/flip is what it sells for on your world, after tax, less the buy price: the 30-day average, or the cheapest listing already on your world when that is lower, since you would have to undercut it.

Worth knowing

Rare items are where listings lie most, so profit rests on real sales, but never above what your world is already listing it for. caps beside Sell now marks a row priced at that listing, and ⚠ one nothing has sold on your world in 30 days, which the headline card leaves out. Min profit and Min avg price cut out small flips (they start at 1M and 2M, which hides most rolls, facewear and dyes: lower them, or pick a Category, to see those), and Hide same-world deals leaves only the ones that need a hop.

Buy = cheapest NQ listing anywhere on Chaos (world shown in orange = hop required, teal = already on your world). Sell avg 30d = 30-day average sale price on your home world — more reliable than current listings for rare items. Profit = the lower of Sell avg and Sell now, × (1 − tax) − Buy: you have to undercut what your world already lists (Sell now is ignored when the cheapest listing anywhere is on your own world, since that is the one you buy). Margin = Profit ÷ that sell price. Units/day = how many units actually sell a day on your home world over 30 days — a stack of 99 counts as 99, not as one sale. Gil/day = net sale price × units/day — how much gil the item moves on your world, which is the market’s pace rather than your cut of it. Data via Universalis.
