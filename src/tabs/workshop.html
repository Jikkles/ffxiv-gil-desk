What it works out

All 162 Free Company workshop projects (submersible and airship parts, housing exteriors, aetherial wheels), costed from turn-ins bought on your data centre against what the finished project sells for on your world.

Reading the table

Click a project for its phases, each turn-in with how many sets it needs, and the recipe under every craftable turn-in. Precraft-optimised crafts any turn-in cheaper to make than to buy; Buy turn-ins prices them all off the board. Either quality is accepted, so each turn-in is priced at the cheaper of NQ and HQ, or at an NPC's price where one sells it for less.

Worth knowing

Drafts, company credits and workshop time are not gil, so they are not counted. Big projects sell rarely: a ⚠ marks one with no sales in 30 days, and those are kept out of the headline cards. Shards and crystals are costed but hidden under each recipe; tick Show crystals to list them.

Projects are read from the game’s own CompanyCraftSequence, CompanyCraftPart, CompanyCraftProcess and CompanyCraftSupplyItem tables, so every phase, set size and set count is exactly what the workshop asks for. Recipes under each turn-in come from Teamcraft’s public data. Mat cost counts every turn-in across every phase; under Precraft-optimised a turn-in that is cheaper to craft than to buy is costed as crafted, recursively, including its crystals. Turn-ins accept either quality, so each is priced at whichever of NQ and HQ is cheaper, and a turn-in or material an NPC sells for gil all year is bought from the NPC when that beats the board. A project also needs its draft (bought with company credits) and a workshop, and several members can hand in at once — none of that costs gil, so none of it is counted here. Finished projects only ever sell NQ. Gil/day = profit per project × the units that sell a day on Spriggan over 30 days — a ranking, not a promise: these are slow, lumpy markets. Data via Universalis.
