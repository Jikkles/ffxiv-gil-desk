What it works out

Drops that sell for real money from dungeons, deep dungeons, variant and criterion, Eureka, Bozja and Occult Crescent, with how often each one actually drops, from crowd-sourced records of thousands of runs and coffers.

Reading the table

Chance is per run for a dungeon (every chest added together, and you still have to win the loot roll), otherwise per coffer or sack. Expected is what one run, coffer or sack is worth from that item: chance × average price after tax. Exchanges show their currency cost instead, and Expected becomes gil per unit of currency.

Worth knowing

Rare drops sell a few times a month on any one world, so Sell now is your world and everything else is measured across your data centre. Narrow it with Content and Obtained by. A few FATE drops have no recorded rate, and say so.

Where the rates come from. Drop chances are read from Infi’s crowd-sourced loot records (updated recently), summed across every patch each coffer has been recorded in: deep dungeon sacks, Eureka lockboxes and bunny coffers, Bozja lockboxes, Occult Crescent treasure, pot and bunny coffers, and dungeon chests. A dungeon’s chance is per run — every chest in the duty added together — and is the chance the item appears, not that you win it: in a party it still goes to a loot roll. A deep dungeon or field coffer chance is per coffer or sack. Exchanges show the currency they cost instead, read from the game’s own shop tables, and their Expected figure is gil per unit of currency. A handful of notorious-monster FATE drops, Cassie Earring and Blitzring among them, have no recorded rate at all and say so. Only drops that sell for real money are listed — roughly 40k and up from dungeons, variant exchanges and field operations, 100k and up from deep dungeons, whose sacks are otherwise mostly glamour weapons — so the everyday materia and potions are left off. An item with several sources shows the likeliest one, and the rest on hover. Sell now = cheapest listing on Spriggan. Avg 30d, Trend, Units/day and Gil/day are measured across Chaos, because a rare drop sells a handful of times a month on any one world. Gil/day = Avg 30d × (1 − tax) × Units/day. Data via Universalis.
