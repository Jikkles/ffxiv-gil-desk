What it works out

Pick a currency from the row of icons and it lists every marketable item that currency buys, with what each sells for on your world after tax. The ⭐ tile shows the best buy for every currency at once.

Reading the table

The chip on the right of each item is what one costs in that currency. The Best rate card is the gil one unit of currency turns into: that is the item to spend on. Value at picks the sale price used: Avg 30d (steadier, the default), Current lowest, or Conservative, the lower of the two.

Worth knowing

A great rate on something that sells once a month is not much use, so Min units/day hides slow sellers; set it to Any to see a whole shop, which the potsherd currencies need. Tick Tribal currencies to add those to the icons. Every row names its vendor, and a few belong to NPCs no longer in the game.

Pricing every currency item…

Costs are read from the game’s SpecialShop and GCScripShopItem tables, rebaked after every patch and filtered to items that are actually sellable on the market board. Where the same item is sold by more than one vendor for the same currency, the cheapest is used. Tomestone and scrip costs use the client’s virtual-currency indices, resolved the same way the CurrencySpender plugin does it. Sell now is the cheapest current listing on your world; Avg 30d is the recent average sale price — steadier for thin items, which is why it is the default basis. Net is that price after market tax. Some shop rows belong to vendors that no longer exist in-game, so the vendor name is shown on every row — if it looks like a dead NPC, treat the row with suspicion. Data via Universalis.
