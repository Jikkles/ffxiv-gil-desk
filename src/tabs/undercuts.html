Undercuts

Every listing your retainers have up on Spriggan, found by reading the whole market board, with the ones someone has undercut first: by how much, and how many listings now sit below yours.

ⓘ Only listings Universalis has been sent show up, so one you put up recently may not appear until that item's board is opened again with an uploader running.

What it works out

Add your retainers' names and press Check for Undercuts (or Refresh), and the tab reads every listing on your Sell on world, about seventeen thousand items, and picks out the ones your retainers put up. A listing is undercut when someone else is selling the same item, at the same quality, for less.

Reading the table

Your price is the price per unit your retainer is asking, with the stack size; identical stacks are folded into one line. Undercut says how far the cheapest rival is below you and how many listings are cheaper, or Cheapest when nobody is. Sell now is the cheapest listing of that quality on your world, yours included, with how old that data is. Your own retainers never count as undercutting each other.

Worth knowing

The market board data comes from players whose plugins upload what they see, so a listing is only as fresh as the last time someone looked at that item. Busy items are usually minutes old; quiet ones can be days old, and the age on Sell now says which is which. The game doesn't share who owns a listing, only the retainer's name, so your character name won't find anything. A retainer with a dashed outline has no listings on this world, or its name is spelt differently.

ⓘMissing a listing? This only shows what Universalis has seen. Anything you put up since an item was last uploaded won't appear until someone opens that item on the market board with an uploader running (Dalamud's is on by default), so open it yourself and check again. The age on Sell now is how old each item's data is.
