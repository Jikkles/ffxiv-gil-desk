What it works out

Profit on 9,400+ things you can craft, precrafts included: what each sells for on your world, less tax, less what its materials cost across the data centres in Mats from. That is thousands of prices, so this tab only scans when you press Refresh. Pick your Class and a level range to see only what you can make, and tick Precrafts only for intermediates, the crafts that go into other recipes.

Reading the table

Mat cost assumes you craft any intermediate that is cheaper to make than to buy (Precraft-optimised); switch to Buy all mats to price everything off the board. Open a row for the whole recipe, buy or craft at every step, and the world to buy each material on. A material an NPC sells for less than the board asks, or that has no listing, is bought from the NPC: the tree says NPC and the zone, and hovering names the vendor. Shards and crystals are costed but left out of each recipe to keep it short; tick Show crystals to list them. Sell prices each item at the quality it usually trades at; plenty of intermediates only really sell NQ, so switch it to NQ to find that market. An open recipe also shows the best time to sell: the hours of the day its sales land in.

Getting the most from it

Tick Hide no-sales and sort by Profit/unit or Profit/day. Profit/day is your cut — profit per unit times how fast the item sells — so it ranks a small margin that shifts constantly above a big one that never moves. It is not the same number as Gil/day on the tabs where nothing is bought with gil — a dungeon drop, a gathered node, a venture reward — which is the market's whole turnover, because there no cost comes off. Hide ⚠ outliers starts ticked, so prices far from real sales stay out of the way. Skip dead items leaves out anything unsold for 30 days and checks each one again a week later, for each world on its own; shift-click Refresh to check them all now. Profit/day = profit/unit × the units that sell a day on Spriggan over 30 days — a stack of 99 is 99 units and one sale, which is why Units/day and the sale count beside Avg 30d are different numbers. You capture a slice of that, so it's a ranking not a promise. Sell now = cheapest current Spriggan listing (⚠ = far from the 30-day average, likely noise). Mat cost defaults to Precraft-optimised: every intermediate that is cheaper to craft than to buy is costed as crafted, all the way down the tree. Switch it to Buy all mats to price every direct material straight off the board instead, with craftable intermediates priced HQ and raw mats NQ. Either way, a material an NPC sells for gil all year is costed at the NPC's price wherever that beats the board, or the board has none (NPC stock is NQ). In the tree, each craftable node shows HQ buy vs craft-it-yourself, and the Precraft-optimised total is what you'd pay crafting the cheaper intermediates.
